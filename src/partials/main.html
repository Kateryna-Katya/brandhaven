GAME OVERVIEW

Puzzles with a sense of adventure

Royal Adventure offers a familiar match-3 core enhanced by a fantasy setting. Each completed level contributes to restoring locations and unlocking new areas across the kingdom. You can actually see your progress - it's pretty satisfying watching the kingdom come back to life.
